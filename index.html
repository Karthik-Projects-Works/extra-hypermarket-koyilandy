📍 Where is Extra Hypermarket located in Koyilandy?

Extra Hypermarket is located directly on the Koilandi National Highway at FM3P+828, Highway, Kollam, Koyilandy, Kerala 673307 (also recorded as 42/90A, Kollam, Viyyur). It features easy highway entry and spacious dedicated parking.

⏰ What are the hypermarket opening hours?

We are open every day from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, all 7 days of the week, including Sundays and public holidays.

Yes! We have an expansive, free dedicated parking lot right in front of the store with capacity for 50+ cars and two-wheelers.

We accept all UPI applications (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa/Mastercard/RuPay Credit & Debit cards, Sodexo/Meal Cards, and Cash at all 8+ billing counters.
